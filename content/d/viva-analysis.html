Viva and six direct competitors: audience, content, ads, search and AI answers

A competitive analysis by Daniel Forero, collected November 19, 2026. US market, English. Viva compared with Athena, BELAY, Boldly, Magic, Persona and Prialto. Public data only.

Viva has more owned audience than most of its competitors and some of the best proof in the category: clients like Notion, coverage in Fortune and Forbes, a real mission and 240+ EAs. In the week measured it published nothing on any channel, it runs no Google or Meta ads, and ChatGPT names it in 3 or 4 of 25 buyer answers. The data also shows that ad spend does not buy AI visibility. Magic spends an estimated 246K USD a month on Google and is named in none of the 25 answers, while Boldly spends nothing and is named in all 25. What moves AI answers is volume and third-party proof, and those are the assets Viva already has and is not using.

3. Press, podcasts and borrowed audiences

3.1 Viva's press

AP, Fortune (June 22, 2026), Fast Company, Forbes, Inc., the Zapier blog (two pieces) and European Business Magazine. Adnan writes for Unite.ai and Fineas for the Turing blog. It is strong coverage, but it arrives one piece at a time, without a program that keeps it coming.

3.2 BELAY and Ramsey

Ramsey is not a BELAY sub-brand. BELAY sponsors Ramsey Solutions' EntreLeadership podcast and uses that audience.

Since when
BELAY's landing page says it has served over 574 "friends of EntreLeadership" across 48 states since 2018.
How it works
A host read on the show with a short URL and a text code, a co-branded landing page with quotes from Dave Ramsey, a free consultation and a free resource. BELAY repeats the formula with Maxwell Leadership (77 clients since 2022, by its own count).
In the ads
BELAY runs Meta ads built on the partnership, with a Ramsey Solutions guest and a "Listen Now" call to action.
Reach
Ramsey's media kit claims 20M+ weekly listeners across its network.

5.2 Viva's organic traffic

Viva ranks for 976 terms and earns about 1,040 estimated visits a month. It ranks well for buyer terms, but those terms carry little traffic: "virtual executive assistant" in position 2 (about 99 visits), "executive assistant services" in position 3 (about 21), "virtual executive assistant service" in position 1 (about 15). A large share of the rest comes from the wrong intent: "data entry virtual assistant", "what is an executive assistant", definitions, Viva careers and EA jobs, and searches for an unrelated company with a similar name.

5.3 What Viva has paid for on Google

"executive assistant" (11,300 searches a month, almost all job seekers), "recruiter near me", "work from home job", "virtual assistant job(s)", "recruitment agency", "belay solutions", "hire virtual assistant" and "virtual executive assistant". Recruiting campaigns and buyer campaigns have been mixed together.

5.4 One company, four names

Viva Talent (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, the March 2026 logo), "Viva, Executive Assistants" (X), Exec Viva Inc. (Google and LinkedIn ads) and execviva.com. "Viva" also collides with Microsoft Viva and other companies in search results and ad libraries.

6. AI answers

The same five buyer questions for every company, asked to ChatGPT five times each, US, English. The questions ran through the DataForSEO API in clean sessions, not from a personal account, so no IP address, history or memory could shape the answers.

  1. What are the best executive assistant services for busy executives in the US?
  2. Which companies are best for hiring a dedicated remote executive assistant for a C-level executive?
  3. What are the top executive assistant services for tech startups and scale-ups?
  4. I'm a CEO drowning in email and meetings. Which service can give me a dedicated executive assistant?
  5. Which remote executive assistant companies are the best alternatives to hiring an in-house EA?

Other companies ChatGPT named: Double (14 to 16 answers), Time Etc (14 to 17), Wing (7 to 10) and Zirtual (4 to 11). The sources it cited most were prialto.com (38 citations), basehq.com (33), boldly.com (30), belaysolutions.com (29) and athena.com (20). execviva.com is not in the top 10.

The earlier, larger measurement on this site points the same way: ChatGPT named Viva in 3 of 219 buyer answers and BELAY in 180. execviva.com was cited in 49 answers but almost never recommended.

7. What it adds up to

Ad spend does not buy AI visibility. Magic spends about 246K USD a month on Google and is named in 0 of 25 answers. Boldly spends nothing and is named in 25 of 25, with daily content, 245 videos and its own site cited as a source. Prialto, at about 1.3K USD a month, is the most cited source of all.

Viva does not need to outspend BELAY or Magic. Its gap is volume and third-party proof: posting to the audience it already has, turning its clients, press, EAs and mission into content others cite, and getting into the lists and review sites AI assistants already read. That is the starting point of the 90-day plan in the R2 deck.

Method

Size (1)
Each company's LinkedIn "About" page, read November 19, 2026.
Social (2)
Only accounts linked from each company's website. Follower counts read on each platform. Posts in the last 7 days: LinkedIn by relative date on the loaded posts; Instagram by the date encoded in each post, excluding old pinned posts; X by each post's date; YouTube long videos only, because Shorts do not show a date in the grid; TikTok by the date encoded in each video ID. 90-day LinkedIn cadence and average likes from a LinkedIn post data provider.
Press and podcasts (3)
Public web sources, each company's landing pages and published media kits. Partner client counts are the companies' own claims.
Ads (4)
Facebook pages taken from each website's footer, with the page ID confirmed on Facebook. Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center and LinkedIn Ad Library, worldwide, all ads, with LinkedIn advertisers confirmed by logo and "paid for by". Google "live today" uses the Transparency Center's Today filter applied in the interface and checked on screen, US. Each ad classified by its copy and call to action. Google spend: SpyFu domain estimates for October 2026, which cover the whole domain and tend to run high.
Search (5)
DataForSEO Google Ads search volume and CPC, US. Viva's rankings and estimated visits from DataForSEO Labs. Paid keywords from SpyFu.
AI answers (6)
ChatGPT through the DataForSEO API, clean sessions, US, English, the five questions above, five runs each, one report per company. A company counts as named when its name appears in the answer. DataForSEO is the data provider behind many SEO platforms.
