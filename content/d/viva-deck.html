Part 2 · Earned media (C)

Earn media with data nobody else has, and put the co‑CEOs on stage

Four reports a year

  • "2026 State of the EA: is AI replacing executive assistants?" Where it is happening, where it isn't, how. Clients, prospects, Viva's EAs and public data.
  • The Executive Time Report: hours lost per role. Builds on the 300+ client survey (9+ hours a week saved).
  • The impact report: 1,000 meaningful jobs for women in LatAm.
  • A cut per event, e.g. what security leaders delegate, launched before RSAC.

Why it compounds

Journalists get data they can't get elsewhere. LLMs index the report and cite Viva in conversations it isn't part of today: "how do I replace this role with AI", "how do I monitor operations with AI".

Speakers

Fineas, Adnan, myself, or anyone at Viva who can speak with authority, media-trained. Stage, panels and partner side events at 4 of the 6 committed events.

How

Exclusive with one top outlet, then wide. The team pitches week to week; a PR agency runs 3 sprints for the big launches.

Appendix · Event funnel and sales handoff

From registration to closed-won, with a handoff to sales at every step

  • Registration: scored in minutes as ICP, partner or other. Not ICP is re-scored as a potential partner.
  • Approval: the event's own standard; a room that is 50 to 60% ICP; overbook by expected show rate.
  • MQL: ICP by company and role. SQL: an MQL that confirms 2 of 3 in a human touch (executives to support, timing, current pain).
  • Routing: 201-500 employees to an AE; 2,000+ to the Head of Strategic Accounts; partners stay with marketing.
  • SLA: sales first touch within 1 business day; outcome in HubSpot within 24 hours; an SQL with no meeting in 30 days goes back to marketing nurture.
  • Feedback: a weekly 15-minute sync; lost reasons feed content and the next event's form.
